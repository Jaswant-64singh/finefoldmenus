About Waterproof Menu Covers (NTR)

Premium Protection Without Compromising Style

Waterproof Menu Covers (NTR) are crafted for hospitality spaces where menus are handled constantly and exposed to moisture, humidity, spills, and outdoor conditions. Built with a specialised waterproof surface, they keep your menu presentation crisp, clean, and professional service after service, without the wear, warping, and staining of conventional covers.

Every cover is finished with premium materials and refined detailing, so protection never comes at the cost of presentation. From matte and gloss surfaces to embossed logos and metal corner protectors, each piece is tailored to reflect your brand while delivering dependable, long-lasting performance in the busiest environments.
