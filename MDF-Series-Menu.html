About MDF Series Menu Covers

The Perfect Blend of Durability, Style & Customization

Our MDF Series Menu Covers are crafted using premium engineered MDF boards that deliver strength, stability, and a refined structure suitable for daily hospitality use. Each cover is finished with carefully selected surface materials that elevate the presentation of your menu while protecting it from the wear of a busy service floor.

From compact café menus to grand fine dining presentations, every cover is made to order — sized, finished, and branded to your specification, so your menu becomes a natural extension of your interior and your brand identity.

Why Choose MDF Series Menu Covers?

Designed for Performance. Crafted for Lasting Impressions.

Every detail of our MDF Series is engineered for the realities of hospitality service — constant handling, changing menus, and the need to look immaculate at every table, every evening.

Strong & Durable Construction

Built on a high-density MDF core that resists bending, warping, and daily wear, keeping your menu covers rigid and presentable through years of service.

Premium Decorative Finishes

Choose from wood grain, leather, laminate, acrylic, fabric, and coated surfaces, each finished with refined detailing that complements your interior styling.

Fully Customized Branding

Logo embossing, laser engraving, foil stamping, and UV printing allow your brand to be presented with precision and consistency on every single cover.

Practical & Long Lasting

Replaceable inserts, easy-clean surfaces, and protective edge finishing make everyday menu updates effortless while extending the life of every cover.
